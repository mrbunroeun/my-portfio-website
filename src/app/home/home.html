Full-Stack Multi-Vendor Lifecycle

1
Storefront Discovery

Customers browse modern store templates, multi-category galleries (Skincare, Apparel, Accessories), and dynamic product lists.

2
Vendor Onboarding Application

Sellers click "Create Your Store" in the hero and submit a multi-step questionnaire (customer location, photos readiness, delivery).

3
Admin Verification & Gating

Submitted vendor profiles enter a secure review table. Stores remain pending until platform administrators verify and approve them.

4
Shopping Bag & Express Checkout

Shoppers add items to bag with real-time cart recalculation and pay via ABA Pay, Bakong / KHQR, or card.

5
Customer Orders Ledger & Tracking

Transactions write directly to MySQL with transactional safety; customers view order history (quantities, dates, vendor breakdown, totals).
